This standalone edition contains Chapter 4 of the dissertation. References to
Chapter 3 concern its conductance-based interpretation of ion channels as
memristive fading-memory elements. The membrane equation cited as (3.3) in the
HH settings table is reproduced here from that chapter:
\begin{equation}
C_m \dot V
=
-\bar g_{\mathrm K} n^4 (V-E_{\mathrm K})
-\bar g_{\mathrm{Na}} m^3 h (V-E_{\mathrm{Na}})
-\bar g_L (V-E_L)
+ I_{\mathrm{ext}}.
\tag{3.3}\label{eq:HH_voltage_recalled}
\end{equation}
Here $m$ and $n$ are activation variables and $h$ is an inactivation variable.
Their voltage-dependent kinetics and numerical parameters are given in the
channel equations and settings tables of Chapter 4.
